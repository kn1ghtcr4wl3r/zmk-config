## Layer Activation

| Layer | Name | Activation | Key |
|-------|------|------------|-----|
| 0 | Default | Always Active | - |
| 1 | Navigation | Hold Left Thumb (1st) | **ESC** |
| 2 | Symbols | Hold Right Thumb (1st) | **BSPC** |
| 3 | Numpad | Hold Left Thumb (2nd) | **SPACE** |
| 4 | Function | Hold Right Thumb (3rd) | **DEL** |
| 5 | Mouse | Hold Left Thumb (3rd) | **TAB** |
| 6 | Media | Hold Right Thumb (2nd) | **RET** |

## Legend

- **hm LCTRL/LALT/LSHFT/LCMD**: Homerow mods - Hold for modifier, tap for key
- **th N ESC/SPACE/TAB/RET/BSPC/DEL**: Thumb hold-tap - Hold for layer N, tap for key
- **BT_SEL 0-4**: Select Bluetooth device
- **BT_CLR**: Clear Bluetooth pairing
- **SCRL_UP/DOWN/LEFT/RIGHT**: Mouse scroll
- **MOVE_UP/DOWN/LEFT/RIGHT**: Mouse movement
- **LCLK/MCLK/RCLK**: Left/Middle/Right click
- **C_PREV/STOP/PLAY/NEXT**: Media controls
- **BRI_UP/DOWN**: Brightness up/down
- **VOL_UP/DOWN**: Volume up/down
- **C_MUTE**: Mute
